# Safa privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Safa is a prayer times, Quran and Qibla app. It has no accounts, no
advertising and no tracking, and it never sends your location anywhere.

## What stays on your device

- **Location.** If you allow it, your position is read to calculate prayer
  times and the Qibla direction. The calculation happens on your device. Your
  position is never sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Settings, bookmarks and reading position.** Stored on your device only.
  Deleting the app deletes them.
- **Quiet time (Android).** If you switch it on, the app changes your phone's
  sound mode at the times you set. Nothing about this leaves your device.

## What leaves your device

The app works offline. When online, it contacts the update service run by
Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) to check for a newer version of itself. That
request contains:

- **a random installation identifier.** It is generated on the device, is not
  derived from your hardware, your Apple or Google account or any other
  account, and is used to deliver
  updates and to count how many installations received one;
- **update diagnostics.** If an update fails to start, the identifiers of the
  failed updates and a short error message are sent so the fault can be found;
- **technical details of the app**, such as its version and platform.

The request is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Like any internet
request, it also reveals your IP address to the server.

It never contains your name, your location, your bookmarks or anything about
what you read.

Expo stores the installation identifier and IP address with each request, and
keeps error messages for three days. Expo acts as a service provider: it does
not use this data for advertising, combine it with data from other apps, or
pass it to data brokers.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts and no sign-in.
- No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
- No sale or sharing of data.

## Notifications

Prayer alerts are scheduled on your device. No notification server is involved.

## Children

The app contains nothing unsuitable for any age and is meant for a general
audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children,
or from anyone: the only information that leaves the device is the update
information described above.

## Deleting your data

Everything personal is on your device and is removed when you delete the app.
The installation identifier cannot be linked to you, so there is nothing we
can look up or remove on request.
