# Safa support

Safa shows prayer times, the Quran with an English translation and
transliteration, and the Qibla direction.

## Common questions

**The prayer times differ from my masjid's.**
Open Settings and scroll to Fine-tuning. Check the calculation and the Asr
setting, or use "Adjust individual times" to shift a prayer by a few minutes.

**The Hijri date is a day off.**
The app follows the Umm al-Qura calendar. Where the local moonsighting
differs, shift the date under Settings, Fine-tuning, Hijri date.

**Prayer alerts stopped.**
The app queues about twelve days of alerts. Opening it tops them up. Also check that notifications are allowed for Safa in the Settings
app.

**The compass seems wrong.**
Hold the phone flat, away from metal and magnets, and move it in a figure of
eight to calibrate.

**I found a mistake in the text.**
The Arabic is the Tanzil Project's Uthmani text, unaltered. The translation is
Saheeh International as published by QuranEnc. Please report anything that
looks wrong at the link below, with the surah and ayah number.
